> Use case: illustration-story. Asset: striking premium landscape cover for Brindlewood Bay virtual tabletop RPG, 1536x1024. Original painterly editorial illustration, sophisticated mystery paperback cover, fine brushwork and richly atmospheric lighting. Three resourceful women in their sixties and seventies, distinct realistic faces, silver bob with pearl earrings and sage cardigan, curly gray hair with rose scarf, short white hair with dark teal coat, gathered around an oak table with floral porcelain tea cups, an open mystery novel, handwritten clues and reading glasses. They look perceptive, dignified and conspiratorial, not caricatures. Cozy New England coastal bookshop at twilight, lighthouse and misty harbor through the window, an almost imperceptible dark tentacle shape beneath the water. Warm amber interior against deep petrol sea, antique ivory, dusty rose, sage green and brass. Beautiful cinematic composition, women and tea scene in lower half, darker airy upper third for elegant title. Text verbatim in upper third in exquisite large ivory serif typography: 'BRINDLEWOOD BAY'. Small subtitle beneath: 'EL CLUB DE LAS EXPERTAS DEL CRIMEN'. No logos, no watermark, no official art copied.

La taza vectorial es propia. La interfaz está construida con CSS y tipografías de sistema, funciona sin fuentes externas y ofrece un modo de lectura cómoda. Las capturas de QA, cuando se incluyen, proceden de Foundry real y no de una maqueta.
