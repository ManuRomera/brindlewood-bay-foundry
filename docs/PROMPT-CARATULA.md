# Carátula nueva de Brindlewood Bay · prompt para la IA de imágenes

> **Para Manu:** pega **desde `=== EMPIEZA ===` hasta `=== TERMINA ===`** en la IA (ChatGPT con imágenes, Midjourney, etc.). Te dará 3 propuestas; elige una (o dime `redo A` para repetirla con cambios) y pásame el PNG. Yo me encargo de ponerle el logo con la tipografía del sistema, recortarla para la tarjeta de Foundry, y actualizar el sistema, el README y la página del proyecto.

## Qué copiar de Legend in the Mist (y qué no)
- **Sí:** ilustración pintada de personajes con carácter, luz dramática, un solo tema cromático muy marcado, y un logo grande integrado en la imagen. Se lee al primer vistazo, incluso pequeña.
- **No:** su fantasía rústica. Brindlewood es **un misterio «cozy» con horror cósmico**: señoras mayores, té y pastas, un pueblo de costa de Nueva Inglaterra… y algo enorme y antiguo asomando por debajo.

## Formato de la tarjeta de Foundry
Foundry recorta la imagen **desde el centro** a unos **1,85 : 1** (aprox. 630×340) y pone **el título arriba** y **cuatro botones abajo a la derecha**. Por eso el prompt pide:
- Imagen **panorámica 16:9** (3200×1800 o más).
- Todo lo importante (rostros, manos, cartas, la criatura) en la **banda central** (entre el 25 % y el 80 % de la altura).
- **Parte superior (~25 %) y esquina inferior derecha tranquilas** (cielo, niebla, agua): ahí van el título y los botones.
- **Sin texto en la imagen**: el logo lo compongo yo, para que no salga mal escrito.

=== EMPIEZA ===

# ROLE
You are the lead illustrator of a tabletop role-playing game called **"Brindlewood Bay"**: a *cozy cosmic* murder-mystery game. The players are **a club of older women who solve murders in a quiet seaside New England town, using tarot cards**, while something vast and ancient (the Void) hides beneath the town. Tone: warm, witty, slightly uncanny. Think *Murder, She Wrote* meets Lovecraft: tea and cake in the foreground, a hint of cosmic dread in the background. Never gory, never grim, never parody.

# TASK
Create **three alternative key-art covers** (A, B, C) for the game's box art, in the spirit of the cover of *Legend in the Mist*: a **richly painted, dramatic illustration with strong characters and a bold colour theme**, readable even at thumbnail size. Deliver each as a PNG, **16:9, at least 3200×1800**. Wait for my feedback after delivering the three; if I write `redo A` regenerate only A with my notes.

# COMMON STYLE (apply to all three)
- **Painterly digital illustration** with the polish of modern tabletop RPG box art: confident brushwork, visible texture, soft rim lighting, subtle film grain. Slightly stylised faces, expressive and kind, **never caricature**.
- **Palette:** deep **teal-green and sea-glass** shadows (#1E3A33 to #3F6B5C), **warm amber window light**, **cream** (#EFE3C8) for clothes and china, with **rose** (#C48A8A) and **plum** accents. A few tiny touches of **eerie violet** from the Void. One dominant colour theme: teal.
- **Cast:** a club of **four women aged 60–85**, varied in age, build and heritage, each with a distinctive look (cardigans, brooches, reading glasses on chains, a silk scarf, a straw hat, a cane, a knitting bag). They look **competent, amused and a little too curious**.
- **Tarot:** the women use **tarot cards** to solve crimes. Show a few cards with **simple clear symbols, no letters or numbers**: a tower struck by lightning, a crescent moon, a skeleton with a scythe, an eight-pointed star.
- **The Void:** something huge and ancient suggested, not shown fully: **tentacles** curling out of the sea behind the harbour, a **faint giant eye** in the cloud bank, or the town's reflection in the water slightly wrong. It must feel **quietly ominous, not monstrous**.
- **Town:** Brindlewood Bay, Victorian seaside houses with gingerbread trim, a lighthouse, a harbour with small boats, dusk or twilight, lit windows.
- **Composition for a wide card:** important elements in the **central band** (25 %–80 % of the height); the **top ~25 %** and the **bottom-right corner** are calm (sky, mist, water) because a title and four buttons will be placed there; keep a quiet area on the **left third** or **right third** where a large logo will sit.

# THE THREE COVERS
**A · The club portrait.** The four women stand together in the right two-thirds, at a slight low angle, facing the viewer: one holds a fan of tarot cards, one a steaming teacup and saucer, one a magnifying glass and a knitting bag with needles, one a small notebook and a pencil. Behind them the harbour at twilight, the lighthouse beam crossing the sky, and **a vast tentacle rising out of the bay, small and far, tinted violet**. The left third stays open for the logo (dark teal sea and sky).

**B · The tarot table.** An intimate scene in a cosy Victorian parlour at night: the four women around a round table under a hanging lamp, a **tarot spread** on the lace tablecloth with a tower card and a moon card catching the light, teapot, cups, slices of cake, a ball of wool. Through the big bay window behind them, the harbour at night where **an enormous eye of faint violet light opens in the clouds** and tentacles move under the water. Faces lit warmly from below; the room amber, the window teal and violet. Calm upper area (ceiling shadow and curtains).

**C · The cliff-top view.** Wide establishing shot: the four women small in the lower left, seen from behind on a path, one holding a lantern and one a teacup, looking out over Brindlewood Bay at dusk. The town below with glowing windows, the lighthouse, and **the sea forming the slow coils of a colossal tentacle and a half-submerged eye in the horizon mist**. A tarot card (the moon) floats faintly in the sky like a second moon. Enormous calm sky in the upper 30 %.
